Notes on the habit-tracking app market

An informal overview. No author, no date, no sources.

Habit-tracking apps help people build routines such as exercise, reading or drinking water. Most of them let the user define a habit, check it off each day and see a streak or a chart of progress.

Many apps in this category offer a free version with limits and a paid version without them. Some charge a monthly or yearly subscription and some charge a single payment. Reminders, statistics, syncing between devices and widgets are common reasons to pay.
